# Gujarati Style Guide

## General Tone
- **Formal & Advisory:** Use the respectful "તમે" (you) and "તમારું" (your) forms. Do not use casual "તું" (thou).
- **Clarity First:** Avoid overly complex Sanskritized Gujarati if a simpler, widely understood term is available.
- **Transparency:** Ensure legal and compliance terms are clearly stated without guarantees.

## Typography
- **Primary Font:** Noto Sans Gujarati.
- **Fallback:** Standard sans-serif stack.

## Terminology & Transliteration

### 3. Placeholders and Variables
- Ensure that text constructed using variables respects Gujarati grammar.
- E.g., Use `{completedCount} / {totalCount} તૈયાર` instead of interpolating into English grammatical structures.
